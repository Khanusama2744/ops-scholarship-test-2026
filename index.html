About the school

Oxford School & College Sundovi

Oxford School & College Sundovi is an educational institution located in Sundovi, Tehsil Puran, District Shangla, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The school provides education from Nursery to Intermediate level, with an emphasis on academic learning, moral values, character development, and students' personal growth.

This website publishes the official result of its Annual Scholarship Test 2026 for students of Classes 3rd to 9th. Every student who appeared in the test receives a class-wise position and an overall position, whether or not they are eligible for a scholarship.

School motto: “Knowledge is Power — Seek Knowledge if it be in China.”
